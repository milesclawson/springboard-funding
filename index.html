What We Do

Grant writing that actually understands public education

Most grant writers know how to write. Springboard Funding Group knows how public schools work — the budget pressures, the equity commitments, the curriculum cycles, and the language that resonates with state and federal funders.

Our founder spent 25 years inside Minnesota school districts, 20 of them as a curriculum coordinator. That insider knowledge is why our proposals are competitive — and why our clients don't have to spend hours explaining context we already know.

Who We Serve

Built for the schools that need it most

We work with Minnesota public school districts, charter schools, and educational cooperatives — particularly those navigating budget pressures, building career and college pathways, or working to expand access for students of color and historically underserved populations.

🏫

Public School Districts

From small rural districts to mid-size metro-area schools, we help build a sustainable grant pipeline and manage grant implementation alongside your existing team.

🌱

Charter Schools

Charter schools often carry big missions and lean budgets. We help identify the right grants for your model and write proposals that communicate your impact clearly.

🤝

Educational Cooperatives

Multi-district collaborations and cooperatives bring unique grant opportunities. We have experience coordinating multi-partner proposals that meet complex funder requirements.
