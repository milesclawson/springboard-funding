Our Work

Grant writing that understands public education from the inside

Springboard Funding Group is a Minnesota-based grant consulting firm dedicated to securing funding for the programs, students, and schools that need it most. We specialize in public education grant writing — from small foundation awards to complex, multi-year state and federal grants — with a consistent focus on educational equity, college and career readiness, and expanding access for historically underserved students in Minnesota public schools.

What sets Springboard apart is not just grant writing expertise. Our founder brings two decades of K-12 curriculum coordination experience, which means every proposal is grounded in real program logic, real student outcome data, and real district context. We don't translate what clients tell us. We already speak the language.

How We Work

Flat monthly retainer. No hourly billing. No surprises.

About the Founder

Miles Lawson brings 25 years of Minnesota public education experience, including two decades in curriculum coordination roles across two metropolitan districts. He has served as grant writer and project manager for more than $1.3 million in awarded public education grants, with additional competitive submissions pending. His curriculum coordination background spans AP and CTE expansion, native language programming, computer science education, work-based learning, gifted and talented programming, and equity-centered course access. His experience with funders spans MN DOE, MN OHE, MN DEED, the Department of Labor, and multiple local and regional sources. Springboard Funding Group, LLC is registered in Minnesota.
