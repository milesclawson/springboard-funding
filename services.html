What grant writing actually involves — and why it matters

A competitive grant proposal isn't just a strong narrative. It requires deep coordination across multiple phases. Here's what Springboard manages on your behalf:

Planning & Research

Identifying the right opportunities, assessing fit with district goals, researching funder priorities, and building the project concept aligned to your strategic framework.

Proposal Development

Drafting a compelling narrative with a clear problem statement, measurable goals, logic model, evaluation plan, and sustainability strategy — all aligned to funder rubrics.

Budget Design

Building detailed, realistic budgets with proper UFARS coding, finance department coordination, and alignment between the budget and the program narrative.

Internal Coordination

Working with your superintendent, director of finance, HR, and program leads to gather sign-offs, supporting data, and letters of support without creating bottlenecks.

Submission & Compliance

Final formatting, submission management, and deadline tracking so nothing slips through the cracks in the final days before a grant closes.

Reporting & Accountability

Supporting interim and final reporting so your district maintains good standing with funders and builds the track record that makes future awards more likely.
