A consistent thread across every grant we write

Every grant in our portfolio shares a common foundation: expanding access for students who have historically been left out — students of color, students with IEPs, English learners, and first-generation college-goers. This isn't a talking point. It's the lens through which we read every RFP, draft every goal, and design every evaluation plan. Minnesota funders at the state level are looking for exactly this — and we know how to demonstrate it compellingly, with your district's actual data.
